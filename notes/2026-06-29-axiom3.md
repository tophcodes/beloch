# 2026-06-29 — axiom 3 landed (v0.2)

`perp .p --l` is in: exact ℚ perpendicular geometry, parsed, evaluated,
and wired through the pipeline. `beloch fold examples/perp.bel` emits 4 faces
with correct `axiom3` provenance. 30+ tests green; `nix build` green.

## What shipped

- `Geom.perpendicular_through : line -> point -> line` — exact rational
  perpendicular through a point, no floating-point, no epsilon.
- `Ast.Perp of point_ref * crease_ref` — the new AST node, with `crease_ref`
  carrying name and span.
- Lexer token `PERP`, parser production `perp crease_ref through point_ref`.
- Eval arm for `Perp`: resolves point + crease names, calls
  `Geom.perpendicular_through`, emits provenance `"axiom3"`.
- `examples/perp.bel`; e2e: fold a diagonal, then `perp --d through .a` → 4 faces.

## Numbering correction

The previous journal note (`notes/2026-06-28-3.md`) called "axiom 3" the angle
bisector (line onto line). Wrong. The authoritative table is [justin1986] §8.1:

- **axiom 3** = operation ③ "Perpendiculaire menée de P à D" — fold through a
  point perpendicular to a line (Hull's O5). **Rational. Landed in v0.2.**
- **axiom 5** = operation ⑤ (D → D') — the fold placing one line onto another
  (the angle bisector; Hull's O4). **First axiom to leave ℚ** (square roots).

The mislabel is recorded in [antipatterns.md](../antipatterns.md) for future
reference. Spec §1, §4.4, §6, and Appendix B updated accordingly.

## ADR 0008 stands

[ADR 0008](../decisions/0008-exact-rational-arithmetic.md) already states that ℚ
closes at axioms 1 and 2; §6 now extends the closed set to axioms 1, 2, and 3.
The ADR's "closure breaks at axioms 5 and 6" is correct and unchanged — that is
the angle bisector and the cubic, exactly as described.

## P ∈ D subtlety

Operation ③ has two fold lines exactly when point `.p` lies on crease `--l`: the
perpendicular through `.p`, and `--l` itself. The second is a trivial
identity-like solution (re-creasing the line produces no new geometry). Decision:
always return the perpendicular. It is unique, it is the meaningful construction,
and the program is almost certainly not asking to re-crease an existing line.
Result: `perp` has no geometric precondition and never errors (beyond
undefined-name errors). This is explicit in spec §4.4.

## Next candidate slice

Three strong options:

1. **Axiom 5 + number representation** — the angle bisector is the next axiom in
   the classic sequence, but it exits ℚ (square roots). These are coupled: axiom
   5 needs a constructible/algebraic number representation decided first. One
   larger slice, higher research value.
2. **Mountain/valley direction** — unblocks folding simulators (Origami Simulator
   can load but not fold our output). Smaller, self-contained; decoupled from the
   number question.
3. **Axiom 4** — projection of a point onto a line parallel to another. Stays in
   ℚ; stretches the axiom set further before the irrational jump.

Mountain/valley is the most immediately useful for demos; axiom 5 is the most
research-interesting. Worth deciding before the next planning session.
