# 2026-06-29 — `Num` landed (constructible reals, v0.3 prep)

`lib/num.ml` is in: exact constructible real numbers, fully tested, no
user-facing change yet. Version is intentionally unchanged — `Num` is an
internal foundation slice with no geometry migration or new axiom this commit.

## What shipped

- `type t = Rat of Q.t | Ext of t * t * t` — `Ext(a, b, d)` = a + b√d, `a b d ∈ Num.t`.
- Full field arithmetic: `add`, `sub`, `mul`, `neg`, `inv`, `div`.
- Exact `sqrt`: collapses perfect rational squares to `Rat`; introduces `Ext`
  only when necessary.
- Exact `sign` (and `compare`, `equal` derived from it): terminates on tower
  height under the canonical generator-ordering invariant.
- `to_float`: output-only, the only float in the numeric core.
- ~30 unit tests covering field axioms, cross-generator products, nested
  radicals, sign edge cases, and `inv`/`div` exact results.

## The v0.3/v0.4 split

The original plan coupled `Num` with axiom 5 (the angle bisector). Splitting
them turned out to be the right call: `Num` is a self-contained mathematical
object with its own correctness story, and getting it right in isolation — before
wiring it into the geometry engine — makes the v0.4 migration straightforward and
reviewable. v0.3 = `Num` in isolation. v0.4 = geometry migration + axiom 5.

## Grounding

The math is grounded in [hull2020] §3.2. Theorem 3.10 characterises the origami
number field as a 2-3 tower of degree-2/3 extensions over ℚ. Lemma 3.9 (with
the degenerate-case argument at ll. 2046–2052) establishes that axiom 5 — the
angle bisector — gives a degree-2-or-less extension. So the quadratic tower
(`Ext` trees, no cube roots) is exactly what axiom 5 needs, and exactly what
`Num` provides. Cube roots arrive at axiom 6 (Beloch's fold, Hull's O7); the `Num` interface is designed to
absorb them later.

## One accepted risk

Deep nesting blows up. Repeated axiom 5 folds can produce `Ext` trees that grow
exponentially in depth. This is purely a speed/size issue — `sign` still
terminates, arithmetic is still exact, results are still correct. Not optimised;
YAGNI until it shows up in practice.
