# 2026-06-30 — Axiom 7 (Justin ⑦, the cubic Beloch fold)

Axiom 7 ships: `map .p onto --d and .q onto --e` (optional `toward .x`).
Simultaneously fold point `.p` onto line `--d` and point `.q` onto line `--e`
with a single crease. This is the last Huzita-Justin axiom.

## Numbering — confirmed

Justin ⑦ = Beloch axiom 7. The numbering coincides at ⑥ and ⑦ (only Hull's
*lower* numbers differ — his O2 is the line-intersection construction, shifting
everything by one). This was already resolved in the axiom-6 slice; axiom 7
required no additional disambiguation. The axiom numbering note in the spec §1
covers both.

## Geometry — the landing-parameter cubic

Each constraint (`.p` onto `--d`) defines a parabola (focus `.p`, directrix
`--d`). The crease must be a common tangent to both parabolas. Parametrising by
the foot of the crease on `--d` gives a **cubic** in the landing parameter
[justin1986 §2], [hull2020 §2.3-2.4]. One or three real roots → one or three
creases. The cubic Beloch fold can double the cube and trisect angles [hull2020
§2.3] — strictly more powerful than ruler-and-compass.

The cubic is solved by `Num.real_roots` via `Mpoly` multivariate resultant
elimination (ℚ-coefficient superset polynomial) + exact separation-bound
certification. The result lives in `ℚ(α)` for an algebraic `α` of degree 3 when
the cubic is irreducible over ℚ; the `Field` kernel then keeps all subsequent
arithmetic bounded (§ shared-field note below).

## Surface syntax

```
map .p onto --d and .q onto --e
map .p onto --d and .q onto --e toward .x
```

`toward .x` picks the solution whose first folded point (image of `.p` on `--d`)
lands nearest `.x` by exact squared distance. Required when the cubic has three
real roots; ignored when it has one.

## Eval

An arm in `eval.ml` mirrors the axiom-6 arm. `Geom.beloch7_creases p d q e`
returns the list of crease lines. The `toward` selector is resolved by nearest
landing (same mechanism as axiom 6). Provenance tag `"axiom7"` on each crease
edge.

## Degeneracy guards

- `.q` already on `--e` → error pointing to axioms 6+4 (the second constraint
  is vacuous; use the appropriate simpler axiom).
- `--d ∥ --e` → error (the cubic degenerates).
- Three solutions without `toward` → ambiguity error naming the selector.
