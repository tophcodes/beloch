---
title: Beloch
description: A declarative language for origami, built on the Huzita–Justin axioms.
---

Beloch is a declarative language for origami. You describe a sheet of paper and
the creases you want in terms of the points and lines on it; Beloch works out the
exact geometry and hands you back a crease pattern and a folded state.

New here? Start with the [tutorial](/tutorials/paper-and-values/) — it builds a
small program up one line at a time. Or try the language right now in the
[Playground](/playground/).
