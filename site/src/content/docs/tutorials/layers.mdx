Every tutorial so far has folded at most once. This one folds twice, and shows
what happens to the paper in between: it stacks. Once there is a stack, a new
fold has to decide how far down it reaches — and `up to` is how you control
that.

## One fold, two layers

Start with a single fold.

<Beloch title="one-fold.bel">
paper square
fold map .d onto .a
</Beloch>

`fold map .d onto .a` folds `.d` (top-left) onto `.a` (bottom-left) — the same
kind of reflection from earlier tutorials, but this time it's a `fold`, not a
`mark`, so the paper actually moves. Toggle to the folded view: the top half of
the sheet is now lying on top of the bottom half. The paper is no longer one
flat face — it is two stacked layers.

## A second fold goes through the whole stack

Fold again, along a new crease.

<Beloch title="two-folds.bel">
paper square
fold map .d onto .a
fold map .c onto .d
</Beloch>

`fold map .c onto .d` creases a new line and folds along it. By default, a
fold goes through *every* layer beneath its crease — top and bottom alike. In
the folded view you can see the result: 4 faces. Both layers from the first
fold got creased by the second.

## `up to` limits the fold to one flap

Add `up to .c` to that second fold.

<Beloch title="up-to.bel">
paper square
fold map .d onto .a
fold map .c onto .d up to .c
</Beloch>

`up to .c` limits the fold to the flap that reaches the anchor point `.c` —
here, just the top layer. The layer beneath is left uncreased. Toggle to the
folded view and count faces: 3, not 4. One layer stayed flat.

`up to <point>` is how you fold "just this flap" instead of dragging the whole
stack along with it — essential once a model has more than one or two layers.

## What you learned

- Folding stacks the paper: after one fold you have layers, not a single flat
  face.
- By default, a fold passes through every layer under its crease — a second
  fold on a two-layer stack creases both layers.
- `up to <point>` restricts a fold to the flap(s) that reach that point,
  leaving the other layers uncreased.

Try adjusting the anchor in the [Playground](/playground/) to see which flap
folds and which stays put.
