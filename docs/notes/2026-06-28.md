## Where the real work starts next

The minimal core (v0.0) is the first actual *thinking*, deliberately tiny:
one square, **axiom 1 only** (`fold .a to .b` → a crease line through two
points), one type (crease line), output = list of creases as FOLD-extended. No
layers, no folded state, no regions, no composition. Goal: `beloch fold` runs
end-to-end on a one-line program.

Open question to chew on before writing `docs/reference/10-core-v0.md`: what *exactly* is
the geometric state the evaluator threads through? For axiom 1 alone it's just
"the paper polygon + a set of crease segments", but pinning the type now, even
minimally, is the design.
